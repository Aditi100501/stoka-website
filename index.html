What is Stoka?

Derived from the Sanskrit word for 'spark', Stoka is a social initiative born from the belief that movement is a universal language.

We teach dance and expressive arts to children and young adults with cognitive disabilities, particularly those from low-income communities. Our sessions are not just about steps; they are about rhythm, autonomy, and the pure joy of taking up space.

Built on radical inclusion and warmth.
