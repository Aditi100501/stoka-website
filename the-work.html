How our program works

Our sessions are more than just classes; they are choreographed journeys of discovery. Every session follows a rhythmic arc designed to safely transition students from their daily world into a state of creative flow.

1

Warm-up

Gentle sensory engagement to ground the body and build awareness of the space and breath.

2

Guided dance

Structured rhythmic movements that build coordination, balance, and motor memory.

3

Free expression

Unstructured time for students to find their own movement language and creative voice.

4

Cool-down

Closing reflections and calming movements to integrate the session's learnings.

Our founding school
