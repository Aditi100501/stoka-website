"Everything you see here happened in one year, in one school, with one class every week."

Photography & Film by Isha Thorat. Isha's role in Stoka goes beyond just documentation; she is a witness to the subtle rhythms of growth. For the past year, she has spent every week with us, becoming a part of the class fabric to capture these candid, joyful moments of physical expression without ever disrupting the flow. Her lens celebrates the 'perfectly imperfect' essence of our movement.
